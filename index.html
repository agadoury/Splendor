How to Play

Goal: First to 15 League Points wins the match.

On your turn, choose ONE:

  • Channel 3 different essences from the supply.
  • Channel 2 of the same essence (only if 4+ remain).
  • Summon a champion by paying their essence cost. Each summoned champion grants a permanent essence bonus.
  • Mark a champion for later and gain 1 Hexgold (wild) essence.

Hold no more than 10 essences. Mark up to 3 champions. When you meet a Faction's essence requirements, they automatically ally with you for 3 LP.

Keyboard: 1-5 essences, Enter confirm, Esc cancel, R marked, ? rules.
