How to Play

Goal: First to 15 prestige wins the saga.

On your turn, choose ONE:

  • Gather 3 different stones from the cosmic supply.
  • Gather 2 of the same stone (only if 4+ remain).
  • Recruit a hero card by paying its stone cost. Each owned hero gives a permanent stone bonus.
  • Reserve a card for later and gain 1 Reality (wild) stone.

Hold no more than 10 stones. Reserve up to 3 cards. When you meet a Team's stone requirements, they automatically join you for 3 prestige.

Keyboard: 1-5 stones, Enter confirm, Esc cancel, R reserved, ? rules.
